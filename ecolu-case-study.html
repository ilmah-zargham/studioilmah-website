DISCOVER

Project context

Ecolu is a mobile shopping concept that helps users make more conscious purchasing decisions without adding friction to the experience.

Instead of overwhelming users with sustainability information, the product focuses on clarity, guidance, and familiar shopping patterns, making eco-friendly choices feel natural within a standard e-commerce flow.

The problem

Sustainable shopping platforms often create friction at the exact moment users need clarity. Some platforms overload users with information, while others simplify too much and reduce trust.

Information overload

Sustainability details are often difficult to compare quickly.

Reduced trust

Oversimplified product claims can remove transparency.

Modern expectations

Users still expect the speed and familiarity of modern shopping apps.

The challenge was to design a system that supports better decisions without slowing users down.

DEFINE

Goals

Calm shopping

Create an approachable shopping experience for sustainable products.

Clear decisions

Make product information easy to scan, compare, and understand.

Less friction

Reduce friction during onboarding and early interaction.

Trust & clarity

Build a visual system that communicates trust and clarity.

Scope

The project covers the core product experience, including onboarding, discovery, search, product detail, cart, checkout, profile, and wishlist.

The focus was on defining a end-to-end flow , rather than isolated screens.

Design system

A lightweight design system was created to ensure consistency across the app.

It includes typography, color usage, components, and interaction patterns designed to support readability and reduce cognitive load.

DEVELOP

Early concepts

Early explorations focused on different onboarding approaches and levels of personalization. Some versions introduced multiple setup steps before entering the app, aiming to improve recommendation quality.

Early onboarding and concept explorations before simplifying the entry flow.

Iteration & onboarding simplification

The initial onboarding flow required users to complete several steps before accessing the product. This created unnecessary friction at the beginning of the experience.

Before

Multiple setup steps before users could enter the main app experience.

After

The flow was simplified to account creation and lightweight interest selection.

Moved later

Additional personalization was moved into the profile section.

Key decision

Prioritize early value over early data collection.

Before: longer registration with too many setup steps.

After: simplified onboarding focused on faster entry.

Flows & information architecture

User flows were mapped to define how users move between key states such as onboarding, discovery, search, cart, and checkout.

This helped ensure:

  • clear transitions between screens
  • predictable navigation
  • minimal dead-ends in the experience

Prototype screens

The prototype translated the structure into a clickable product flow, focusing on onboarding, product discovery, product details, checkout, profile, and wishlist states.

Key prototype screens used to test and communicate the main interaction flow.

Wishlist & saved items

DELIVER

Final UI design

The final interface focuses on hierarchy, readability, and consistency.

Clear presentation

Product information is structured to support quick comparison and easy scanning.

Minimal noise

Spacing, typography, and simplified layouts reduce visual overload across screens.

Predictable interactions

Navigation and interaction patterns remain consistent throughout the shopping flow.

Discovery, onboarding, and home experience.

Product detail, cart, checkout, and order confirmation flow.

Profile, badges, interests, and account settings.

Post-purchase experience

The experience continues after checkout. Users can track their orders, review past purchases, and apply filters to quickly find relevant items.

The goal was to make order tracking feel simple and transparent, reinforcing trust in the platform.

Final prototype flow

The final prototype demonstrates the main shopping journey: starting the app, entering the home experience, opening a product, adding it to the cart, completing checkout, and receiving eco-point feedback after purchase.

Clickable prototype video showing the main Ecolu shopping journey.

Outcomes

This project helped translate a broad concept into a structured mobile product experience, covering both interaction design and visual system development.

Product thinking

UX decisions

Reduced onboarding friction and prioritized early value over early data collection.

System design

Built a lightweight visual system focused on consistency, clarity, and readability.

Key learning

Sustainable experiences should support better decisions without slowing users down.
