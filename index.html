About

I’m a product designer with an engineering background. I enjoy making complex systems feel clear and human, especially when the information is messy or the workflow is intense.

I combine structured thinking with visual sensitivity, from early UX flows and information architecture to UI design and interaction details. I care about accessibility, consistency, and the small moments that build trust in a product. Right now I’m building a portfolio of case studies that show not only the final screens, but also the decisions behind them.
